Privacy Policy for QuickBooks MCP Server

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

Overview

This QuickBooks MCP Server ("the App") is a private, internal tool used exclusively by EverLine Coatings & Services NE Atlanta to connect QuickBooks Online data to Claude AI for automated reporting and data management.

Information We Collect

The App accesses the following data from your QuickBooks Online account:

  • Financial reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet)
  • Transaction data (invoices, expenses, payments)
  • Customer and vendor information
  • Chart of Accounts data

How We Use Your Information

Data accessed through this App is used solely for:

  • Generating financial reports
  • Creating and managing transactions
  • Comparing actual performance to budget
  • Internal business analysis and decision-making

Data Storage and Security

  • OAuth Tokens: Stored locally on the user's computer in encrypted format
  • No Cloud Storage: No QuickBooks data is transmitted to third-party servers
  • Local Processing: All data processing occurs on the local machine
  • No Data Sharing: We do not share, sell, or distribute your QuickBooks data to any third parties

Third-Party Services

This App integrates with:

  • QuickBooks Online: Subject to Intuit's Privacy Policy
  • Claude AI (Anthropic): Used for processing commands and generating insights. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally until manually deleted
  • No QuickBooks data is permanently stored by this application
  • All data queries are performed in real-time against your QuickBooks account

Your Rights

You maintain full control over your data:

  • Revoke access at any time through QuickBooks settings
  • Delete stored tokens by removing the application
  • Request information about data access patterns

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
